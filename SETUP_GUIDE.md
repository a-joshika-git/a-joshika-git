## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Instructions

### Step 1: Create Your Profile Repository
1. Log in to [GitHub](https://github.com).
2. Click **New repository** (or go to `https://github.com/new`).
3. Set the **Repository name** to your exact **GitHub username** (e.g., if your username is `octocat`, name the repo `octocat`).
4. Ensure the repository is set to **Public**.
5. Check **Add a README file**.
6. Click **Create repository**.

---

### Step 2: Customize the Files

Open [`README.md`](file:///C:/Users/ankin/.gemini/antigravity/scratch/github-profile/README.md) and replace these key placeholders:
- `your-username` ➡️ Your GitHub username (used for stats, graphs, and repository links)
- `YOUR-LINKEDIN` ➡️ Your LinkedIn profile handle / URL
- `YOUR-PORTFOLIO-SITE.com` ➡️ Your personal website / portfolio URL
- `YOUR_EMAIL@example.com` ➡️ Your public contact email
- `YOUR-TWITTER` ➡️ Your X / Twitter handle
- Project descriptions and links in the **Featured Projects** table.

To personalize the banner name in [`assets/header-banner.svg`](file:///C:/Users/ankin/.gemini/antigravity/scratch/github-profile/assets/header-banner.svg):
- Open `header-banner.svg` in any code editor.
- Search for `Hi, I'm Your Name` (around line 73) and replace `Your Name` with your real name or handle.

---

### Step 3: Push / Upload to GitHub
1. In your `username/username` repository on GitHub:
   - Upload the `assets/` folder containing `header-banner.svg`.
   - Upload or paste the contents of `README.md`.
   - (Optional) Upload the `.github/workflows/snake.yml` file.
2. Commit your changes to the `main` branch.

---

## 🌟 Pro-Tips for a High-Converting GitHub Profile

1. **Pin Your Top 4-6 Repositories**:
   - Don't leave your pinned repos empty! Go to your profile, click **Customize your pins**, and select your 4 to 6 best projects.
   - Ensure each pinned repo has a clear **About** description, **Topics/Tags**, and a **Website/Demo link**.
2. **Repository READMEs**:
   - Make sure your pinned repos have screenshots/GIFs, quickstart instructions, and architecture overviews.
3. **Consistent Commit Activity**:
   - Regular, high-quality commits keep your stats and streak cards glowing!
